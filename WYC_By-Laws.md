# Articles and Regulations Taking Precedence

## 
Since the Washington Yacht Club (WYC) is a Student Club at the University of Washington (UW), WYC shall abide by and act in accordance with all lawful policies and rules set forth by the UW.

## 
The three governing documents of the WYC are the Constitution, By-Laws, the Policies. Of these, the Constitution has ultimate authority, followed by the By-Laws, and finally the Policies. If anything contained within the By-Laws or Policies contradicts a superseding document it shall be considered null and void. Any rule or decision made by an Officer or Position Holder which contradicts any of these documents is considered null and void.

# Officer Duties
This Article provides details for the duties and powers of the Officers. The definitions of these Officers and procedures for their election and removal from office are included in the Constitution.

## Officer Duties

### Commodores
Presides at all General and Executive Council meetings.

1. Makes appointments and removals of Committee chairpersons, to be approved by the Executive Council.
2. Coordinates all activities of the Club, its Officers, and its Committees.
3. Represents the Club to the ASUW and other members of the University and the general public.
4. Has the power to check the records of the Purser.
5. Insures that all other Officers perform their assigned duties.
6. Sets policies as necessary to ensure Membership is at least 50% Regular Members, in accordance with the Constitution.
7. Owns the Club bank account and authorizes signers as designated by Article VI - Section B.1.a in the By-Laws.

### Vice Commodore
1. Assumes the responsibility of the Commodore in their absence.
2. Organizes and supervises the training program.
3. Supervises the qualifications of all rating qualifications, including the training and qualification of Instructors and Rating Examiners.

### Rear Commodore
1. Assists in the management of the Club in coordination with the Commodores and Vice Commodore(s).
2. Shall work with the publicity chair to coordinate and carry out publicity for the WYC and all WYC events. If the of Publicity Chair is vacant or publicity chair is negligent in their duties it is the responsibility of the Rear Commodore to assume their role till a replacement can be found.
3. Shall work with the Social Chair to plan and hold social events for the WYC. If the position of social Chair is vacant or Social Chair is negligent in their duties it is the responsibility of the Rear Commodore to assume their role till a replacement can be found.

# Executive Council

## Definition
The Executive Council is the Club's planning and coordinating agency. It is responsible to the General Membership.

## Voting Members
Voting members of the Executive Council are Regular Members who are Chiefs, Officers, positions holders, Committee Chairpersons Fleet Captains, and Assistant Fleet Captains.

## Duties of the Executive Council

### Planning and Coordinating
The Executive Council shall act as the Club's planning and coordinating committee, responsible to the General Membership.

### Election of Chiefs
The Executive Council shall elect qualified Skippers to the rank of Chief subject to the approval of the Membership.

### Chief Suspension
May suspend any Member or Chief for a period of time for infraction of the By-Laws or Club Rules.

### Committee Appointments
Will appoint standing committee chairpersons upon the failure of the Commodore to do so.

## Executive Council Meetings
Executive Council Meetings can be called by the Commodore or by the will of the majority of the Executive Council. Adequate notice shall be given to all Members of such meetings.

## Quorum
A quorum shall consist of the Executive Council members present.

# Positions

## Appointment
Positions are appointed by the Commodore with the approval of the Executive Council. All paid positions must be approved by a vote of either the Executive Council or a regular meeting. Commodore may remove Members from a position. Decisions of removal may be overturned by a majority vote by the Executive Council. Additional Positions may be created at the digression of the Commodore and Executive Council.

## Positions
The following are descriptions of Positions in the Club:

### Head Fleet Captain
1. Insures that there is a responsible Member (Fleet Captain or Chief) in charge of all work parties. They are also responsible for the organization and preparation for such work parties.
2. Is responsible for the WAC shop, including coordinating with the Quartermaster to keep it stocked and making sure it is clean.
3. Coordinates the activity of the other Fleet Captains, serves as a communication link between Fleet Captains, and also teaches boat maintenance and repair to other Fleet Captains.

### Fleet Captains
There shall be one Fleet Captain for each class of boat and a Sail Fleet Captain. The Boat and Sail Fleet Captains shall be responsible for the repair and supervision of repair of damaged items within their specific fleets.

1. Are responsible to the Head Fleet Captain and to the Executive Council for the maintenance and repair of their fleet.
2. May remove any Club boat or item of equipment out of service for repair, when damaged, when preventive maintenance is deemed necessary or for work parties.
3. Assistant fleet captains' may be appointed by the Executive Council base on need or by request of a fleet captain.

### Publicity Chairperson
Organizes and coordinates all Club publicity. Including membership drives, open houses, and other recruiting activities.

### Program Director
1. Plans all meetings, lectures, etc., with the Executive Council.
2. Assures that all physical arrangements for the above have been made, e.g.: rooms, films, PA systems.
3. Obtain the University approval, when necessary, for Yacht Club Activities.
4. Is in charge of clerical work for the Club: letters, regatta invitations, meeting notices, minutes of all meetings, etc.
5. Gives Novice and Skipper written examinations during normal office hours in the Club Office
6. Manages Associate Member applications.

### Social Chairperson
Coordinates all social activities with the Executive Council and Vice Commodore, and makes arrangements for places to hold parties, and for food and beverages.

### Snooze'n'Cruise Chairperson
Plans and coordinates all phases of Snooze'n'Cruise with the approval of the Executive Council.

### Quartermaster
Is responsible for maintaining an adequate supply of materials and equipment for boat repair work and for the operation of the Club and coordinates all selling of Club equipment as directed by the Executive Council.

### Purser
1. Coordinates the collection of dues.
2. Reconciles the checkbook register with the monthly bank statement within 30 days of the receipt of the statement.
3. Maintains accounts detailing all income and expenditures and renders accurate quarterly and yearly financial reports as well as brief reports at Executive Council and General Meetings. Quarterly and Annual Reports must be presented in written form to the Executive Council within five weeks of the end of the fiscal period. The Executive Council will have two weeks in which to discuss and approve or disapprove the report(s).
4. Will ensure that financial records divide income and expenditures into useful classifications.
5. Pay bills for Club expenses.

### Tell Tale Editor
Collects information, writes, and distributes the Club newsletter.

### Rating Examiner
1. Administers Novice and Skipper written examinations, and Practical Tests for all ratings they are qualified to give as a Chief or Instructor.
2. The work hours and rate of pay for the Rating Examiner will be set by the Executive Council, subject to the provisions of these By-Laws. Payment will be a flat hourly rate, not dependent of the time of day or day of the week. During work hours, the Rating Examiner will be on duty at the WAC. The Rating Examiner's primary job is to give written and practical tests to Members. When not giving tests, the Rating Examiner will do other work for the Club. This may include boat and facilities maintenance, or any other work the Club requires.
3. The Rating Examiner must be a Chief or Instructor according to the provisions of these By-Laws.

### Assistant Rating Examiner
1. Must have at least one Novice rating and be capable of assisting other Members in the rigging and signing out of boats.
2. May be granted authority to sign check-out sheets as a proxy for the on-duty Rating Examiner while the RE is unavailable, but does not have authority to sign out Members on his or her own.

### IT Specialist/Webmaster
In charge of fixing and maintaining office and sail-locker computer and related hardware and software. In charge of keeping website running and updated.

### Instructors

#### Requirements
To become an Instructor, one must be a Member, have at least one dinghy Skipper rating with an Intermediate rating in another dinghy class, and be approved by the Vice Commodore. Members with the above qualifications but only possessing Single-handed and Double-handed Intermediate ratings may teach in pairs at the Vice Commodore's discretion with the stipulation that they have passed the Skippers Written test and intend to pass the Skippers Practical test as soon as possible. Only Instructors shall be qualified to teach novice and intermediate sailing classes.

##### Sailboard and Catamaran Instructors
The Intermediate or Skipper rating required above must be in the class of boat they wish to teach.

#### Duties
The duties and responsibilities on an Instructor, to be executed with discretion and courtesy, are:

##### Instruction
Instruct Members in beginning and/or Intermediate sailing.

##### Practical Exams
Administer Novice and Intermediate practical examinations.

##### Assistant Instructor Supervision
Supervise the performance of instructional duties by Assistant Instructors.

##### Rescue Barges
Use the Rescue Barge for the purpose of instruction and rating examinations.

##### Written Exams
Administer Novice and Centerboard/Daggerboard Written examinations.

### Assistant Instructors

#### Requirements
To become an Assistant Instructor, one must be a Member, have both a Single-handed and a Double-handed Intermediate rating, be under the supervision of an Instructor who is responsible for training the Assistant, and be approved by the Vice Commodore.

#### Duties
Assistant Instructors are qualified to assist an Instructor in all duties connected with the teaching of their class. They may not perform any of these duties unless the supervising Instructor is physically present.

# Chiefs

## Chiefs

### Requirements
To become a Chief, one must have a Skipper rating, a whaler rating, and be nominated to the position by the Executive Council or by the Members voting at a General Meeting. There will be a probationary period of two quarters during which the Chief designation may be revoked by vote of the Executive Council or of a General Meeting.

### Duties
The duties and the responsibilities of a Chief, to be exercised with discretion and courtesy, are to:

#### Rules Enforcement
Enforce the By-Laws and Executive Policies.

#### Club Authority
Exercise absolute authority in all matters regarding the safety of all Club operations, subject to later review by the Executive Council. A Chief is not authorized to criticize Skippers when they are engaged in recreational sailing except in the case of danger to life, limb, or equipment.

#### Administer Tests
Administer rating tests and assist with the training of Members.

#### Executive Council
Attend and vote at meetings, if they are also Regular Members.

### Expiration
Chief ratings do not expire.

### Officers
The Commodore, Vice Commodore, and Rear Commodore shall automatically become Chiefs upon election if they possess a skipper and whaler rating. They shall be expected to fulfill this requirement as soon as possible and will not have the rights and duties of a Chief until they do so.

## Designated Chief

### Requirements
To become a Designated Chief for a boat class, one must be a Chief, have a Skipper Rating in that boat class, and be designated by a majority vote of Executive Council or a General Meeting.

### Giving Ratings
Only Designated Chiefs can give skipper rating in that boat class.

### Expiration
Designated Chiefs ratings do not expire.

# Financial Matters

## Annual Budget

### Reserve
The Club shall maintain a balance in its primary savings account of at least 30% of the previous year's budget to insure adequate working capital. This reserve may be used in the event that there are not adequate monies in the primary checking account, by approval of the General Membership at a General Meeting.

### Approval
The Purser shall present an annual budget at the first meeting of each winter quarter to be approved by the General Membership. Until an annual budget is approved, no expenditures shall be made, except wages, insurance, existing bills, and fuel.

### Guidelines
An annual budget shall be written by the Purser in accordance with the following guidelines:

#### Due Date
The proposed annual budget shall be submitted for review by the Executive Council no later than December 1st of the previous year.

#### Submission Deadline
Position holders in charge of budgets are invited to submit budget requests to the Purser prior to November 1st of the previous year.

#### Basis
The Purser shall base the budget largely upon revenues and expenditures of the previous year.

#### Input
The Purser shall seek input from no less than two Executive Council members involved with Club expenditures and the Commodore, Vice Commodore, and Rear Commodore in creating their proposed budget.

#### Distribution of Monies
Only 80% of the anticipated revenues shall be distributed in the preliminary budget. The remaining monies shall be at the control of the General Membership.

#### Revision
The Executive Council will make any changes they feel are necessary to the Pursers proposed budget.

#### Publication of Proposed Budget
The Purser will present this revised budget to the General Membership via the wyc_info@u.washington.edu email list 7 days prior to the meeting at which the General Membership shall vote to ratify the budget.

#### Approval
A simple majority vote of Regular Members present shall put the budget into effect.

#### Modification
The budget may be updated at any time at the request of the Executive Council, but must follow the approval procedures outlined for an annual budget.

### Funds

#### Description
The following Funds shall be tracked with revenues being deposited into them from the indicated activities:

##### General
The dues and initiation fees not allocated to the New Boat Fund in addition to any other income not allocated to a different fund.

##### New Boat
Thirty percent of all initiation fees and dues, all unburdened donations, all interest earned on bank deposits, and the proceeds from any sale of Club assets not from the keelboat fleet.

##### Keelboat
Keelboat reservation fees, keelboat sales.

##### Snooze & Cruise
All monies generated from Snooze & Cruise.

#### Carryover
All Funds shall carry over from year to year.

#### Loans
Loans may be made from one fund to another at the discretion of the Executive Council.

#### Redistribution
The Executive Council may redistribute the monies held in any fund at their discretion.

### Categories
Expenditures and budgets shall be tracked based on categories and drawn from funds as specified by Executive Council policy.

### Approval of Exceeding Expenditures
Requests for expenditures exceeding the approved budgeted amount in any category must be approved by the General Membership and posted to the wyc@u.washington.edu email list one week prior to the meeting where the vote will take place.

#### Snooze and Cruise Exemption
Snooze and Cruise is exempted given that the expenditure does not exceed the available funds in the Snooze and Cruise account.

### Emergency Expenditures
Emergency expenses above the budgeted amount in any budget category must be approved by two members of the Executive Council, preferably the Commodore, Purser, or Quartermaster in writing. Documentation of the expenditure amount and approval must be presented at the following General Meeting for approval.

### Reports
Up to date budget monitoring reports shall be presented at every General Meeting by the Purser for review by the General Membership.

### Insurance Costs
Insurance costs shall be paid from the general fund.

## Purchasing

### Check Writing

#### Check Writing Privileges
The following position holders shall have permission to write checks from the Club accounts.

1. Commodore
2. Purser
3. Quartermaster
4. Any Executive Council Designee

#### Distribution
Checks shall be distributed by the Purser upon request.

#### Records
Check stubs shall be turned into the Purser, filled out with receipts detailing which accounts, outlined in Article VI - Section A.4.a of the By-Laws, the purchases shall be taken from.

#### Fees
Any fees associated with determining the details (amount and recipient) of specific checks shall be paid by the writer of the check.

#### Restrictions
Position Holders that have check writing permission, may not write checks related to any other position that they may hold without prior authorization from the Executive Council.

### Purchasing Accounts
The following Position Holders shall have permission to regularly charge on the Club accounts provided they have the Quartermaster's approval:

1. Head Fleet Captain
2. Fleet Captains
3. Commodore
4. Vice Commodore
5. Rear Commodore
6. Snooze and Cruise Chair
7. Quartermaster

### Budget Category Responsibility
Responsibility for individual budget categories shall be specified by Executive Council policy.

### Vacancy
In the event that a position is vacant, the Commodore shall appoint a Member of the Executive Council to preside over the position's designated budgets while a new position holder is being sought.

### Authorized Expenditures
Position Holders shall be authorized to spend their approved quarterly budget, or authorize the quartermaster or responsible Member in their stead.

#### Spending Cap
Any single item exceeding $1000 shall be approved by the General Membership, and any single item costing between $500 and $1000 must be approved by either the General Membership or any two Commodores, Vice Commodores, or Rear Commodores.

### Purchasing Procedures

#### Purser Notification
The Purser must be notified of all expenses and the budget that each expense is to be charged to.

#### Receipts
Receipts with multiple items from different budgets must clearly indicate the portion that is to be allocated to each budget.

#### Forms
To facilitate the above, a WYC Purchasing Form shall be utilized with the approval of the Executive Council as to its layout.

# Club Document Amendments

## Constitutional Amendments
Procedures for amending the Constitution are described in the Constitution.

## Amending the By-Laws and Policies
The By-Laws and Policies may be adopted, amended, and revised by a majority vote at a General Meeting called according to the procedures outlined in Article VII - Section C of the By-Laws where a quorum is present.

## Procedure

### By-Laws
A proposed By-Laws amendment must be announced at a General Meeting where discussion and revision of the amendment will take place. The revised amendment will then be posted in an appropriate manner to inform the Members at least seven (7) calendar days prior to the General Membership meeting for which the vote is scheduled. Details for this posting are described in the Policies.

### Club Policies
A proposed Policies amendment must be posted in an appropriate manner to inform the Members at least seven (7) calendar days prior to the General Membership meeting for which the vote is scheduled. The amendment may be revised at the same meeting as the final vote. Details for this posting are described in the Policies.

## Announcement
1. After the Constitution, By-Laws, or Policies have been amended, adopted, or revised the Program Director shall incorporate the changes within two weeks (14 days) and post the amended version to the “Club Documents and Forms” page of the Club website.
2. If any document is not updated within two weeks of the changes, those changes shall not be binding until the document has been updated and appropriately distributed to Members.

# Sunset Provision
If an action approved at either a General Meeting or an Executive Council Meeting has not been started within 10 weeks of the vote or the time set for the commencement of that action, that action shall be considered null and void.
