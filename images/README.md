# Windsurf policy maps

The three restored maps below were extracted without redrawing or changing their boundaries from [WYC Windsurf Policies - Windsurf Edits](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NYfwbUFDhq3-PFQTCRsnTHmETRW6XHrT7mzJtVJwuCg/edit), revision August 31, 2024 (last modified November 2025):

- `SSP_northerly_teaching_boundaries.jpg`: northerly teaching boundaries.
- `SSP_northerly_sailing_boundaries.png`: novice and intermediate open-sailing boundaries in northerly winds.
- `Hook_sailing_guide.png`: Hook Park sailing areas, launch, and hazards.

The original document contains five maps. The May 11, 2026 repository import (`d53099e`) included only `SSP_teaching_boundaries.jpg` and `SSP_sailing_boundaries.png`, which show the southerly teaching and sailing boundaries. Those two files are retained unchanged and appear in the southerly policy section.

The restoration retains the current policy text and revision date. Windsurf chiefs should review the recovered maps before the correction is merged.
