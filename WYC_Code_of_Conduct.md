# Scope

This Code applies to all members in club spaces and during club activities, including:

- Club meetings, lessons, social events, work parties, and other WYC-organized activities
- Public online club platforms, such as Discord and email lists
- WYC spaces, including the WAC, Sail Sand Point (SSP), and aboard club vessels

# Expectations

The WYC is a community for learning, teaching, and enjoying sailing. Members are expected to help maintain a welcoming, safe, and constructive environment.

Members should:

- Welcome new members and help them become part of the club
- Treat others with dignity, patience, and respect
- Communicate constructively, including during disagreements
- Give instruction, feedback, and safety warnings without humiliation or personal attacks
- Respect differences in experience, background, perspective, and sailing ability
- Use positions of authority, ratings, experience, and access to club vessels responsibly

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, retaliation, personal abuse, deliberate exclusion, and repeated disruptive or demeaning behavior are not allowed.

Good-faith disagreement, criticism of club decisions, and reporting safety or conduct concerns are not violations merely because they are uncomfortable, unpopular, or strongly expressed.

No code can anticipate every situation. Members are expected to follow both the letter and the spirit of these principles.
